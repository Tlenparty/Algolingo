Sanity check with fontTools: the cmap of both files contains Cyrillic U+0416, U+044F, U+0451, and the files are about 130 KB each.

Write third_party/fonts/README.md (Russian or English, short): the four font files, their upstream sources and licenses (SIL OFL 1.1), and the exact regenerate commands for the two Nunito instances (venv + instancer lines).
